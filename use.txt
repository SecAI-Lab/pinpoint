Intended Use:
- This artifact is provided to reproduce the effectiveness claims of the ACSAC
  2026 paper "Localizing Vulnerabilities under Function Inlining Toward Precise
  Binary Patching" (Tables III and IV).
- It is intended for academic research on vulnerability localization in
  optimized binaries, and for reuse of PinPoint's localization layer over other
  token-based BCSD models.
- PinPoint locates known vulnerable code given a reference for it. It does not
  discover unknown vulnerabilities and says nothing about exploitability.

Public Release:
- Released: the localization layer, the SAFE port, the analysis code, the
  packaged data for all 32 target binaries, the ground truth, and BinShot's
  published weights, under the licenses in license.txt.
- To be released separately with a DOI: the full corpus and the raw outputs of
  the paper's own run.
- Not released: SAFE's weights, which install.sh downloads from the SAFE
  authors' own distribution because SAFE is published without a license; and
  the binaries the data was derived from, since the artifact ships
  static-analysis output rather than programs.

Limitations:
- The packaged subset is 32 of the corpus's 300 target binaries, so results may
  show numerical differences from the paper while showing the same trends. The
  margins are narrower, because a smaller corpus exposes fewer candidate
  functions per binary and so flatters whole-function matching.
- Do not reduce the subset further. Below 32 binaries the Top-5 ordering of the
  two BinShot rows inverts, which the full corpus never does.
- Two of the paper's five model configurations are packaged. Trex needs its own
  preprocessing pipeline, and the graph-based baselines are evaluated at whole
  function granularity only.
- Efficiency results are not reproduced; they are a property of a full-corpus
  run.
- Ghidra and radare2 recovery and DWARF ground-truth extraction are offline
  stages whose output ships as JSON, so the artifact cannot be pointed at a new
  binary as it stands. See provenance.txt.
